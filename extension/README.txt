=====================================================
PHISHCODE AI - GOOGLE CHROME EXTENSION (MANIFEST V3)
=====================================================

HOW TO INSTALL IN GOOGLE CHROME (WINDOWS INSTRUCTIONS):

Step 1: Extract the ZIP Archive
  - Right-click "phishcode-ai-chrome-extension.zip" in your Downloads folder.
  - Select "Extract All..." and click "Extract".
  - You will now have a folder containing manifest.json, popup.html, popup.js, popup.css, and icons.

Step 2: Open Chrome Extensions Manager
  - Open Google Chrome.
  - In your browser address bar, type: chrome://extensions and press Enter.
  - (Or click the 3-dots menu in Chrome -> Extensions -> Manage Extensions).

Step 3: Turn ON Developer Mode
  - In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the switch labeled "Developer mode" to ON.

Step 4: Load the Unpacked Extension
  - In the top-left corner, click the "Load unpacked" button.
  - Browse to and select the extracted extension folder (the folder containing manifest.json).
  - Click "Select Folder".

Step 5: Pin the Extension to Your Toolbar
  - Click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions menu) on the right side of your Chrome address bar.
  - Find "PhishCode AI - Website Phishing Scanner" and click the Pin icon next to it.

Step 6: Scan Any Website With One Click
  - Browse to any webpage (e.g., an online store, banking site, or email link).
  - Click the PhishCode AI shield icon in your Chrome toolbar.
  - Click "Scan This Website" to receive a real-time risk score, warning signs, and safety advice!

PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS:
- Manifest V3 activeTab permission: Activated only when you deliberately click the extension icon.
- Zero credential collection: Does not access cookies, stored passwords, keystrokes, or background browsing history.
- Pure client-side heuristic inspection: Instant, lightweight analysis designed for speed and privacy.
